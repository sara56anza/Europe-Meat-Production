The inflation question

Did inflation after 2022 change production?

The relationship was surprisingly weak.

Countries with stronger inflation did not consistently produce more or less meat.

Correlation: r = 0.092

Conclusion: inflation alone does not explain production changes.

Migration vs production

Migration looked important… at first

Countries with larger immigration inflows also tended to have larger meat production.

Strong raw correlation: r = 0.851

But once the analysis focused on changes over time, the relationship almost disappeared.

Change-based correlation: r = -0.029
One real shift

Poultry is slowly gaining ground

+1.35%

Poultry share increased between 2013–2017 and 2020–2024.

-1.20%

Bovine meat share declined slightly over the same period.

The biggest long-term shift was not total production — it was composition.

Final takeaway

Europe’s meat industry is changing unevenly

  • Production is concentrated in a few countries.
  • Some countries are growing while others decline.
  • Inflation explains little.
  • Migration effects are weaker than expected.
  • Poultry continues to gain share.
